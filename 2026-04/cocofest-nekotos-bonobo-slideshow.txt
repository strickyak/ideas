## NekotOS Slideshow

For a 16K RAM coco2 and a Bonobo 2.4, no MPI.
(* using coco2 "2C" and bonobo 33 or B33 *)


----------------------------------------------------------------------

In one window:

cd ~/modoc/coco-shelf/build-frobio

    ./lemma-waiter  -cards -lemmings_root pizga-base/Internal/LEMMINGS -lan=127.0.0.1 -config_by_dhcp=0 --nav_root pizga --web_static pizga-base/Internal/web-static/ -games_dir pizga-base/Internal/NEKOTOS/ 

In another window:

cd ~/modoc/coco-shelf/copico-bonobo/v2.4/tether

    go run ./. -handle YAK -name strick9 -airport AGS

Then "plug in" the USB cable to the bonobo

Then turn on the coco2.

    /R .Y1

---------------------------------------

cd ~/modoc/coco-shelf/nekotos/data/coco2

Use `sh proc0.sh filenames...` to convert PNG to GNP and vice versa.

Use `sh proc1.sh filenames...` to rename .png files to md5 names.

Use `sh proc2.sh` to convert all *.png for Coco2 framebuffers.
